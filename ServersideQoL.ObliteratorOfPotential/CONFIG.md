<details open><summary><b>ObliteratorOfPotential</b></summary>

|Option|Default Value|Acceptable Values|Description|
|------|-------------|-----------------|-----------|
|Enabled|true|True/False|Enables/disables the entire mod|
|PlaceNearForgeOfPotential|true|True/False|True to place an Obliterator of Potential near the Forge of Potential|
|EveryObliteratorCanUpgrade|false|True/False|True to give every obliterator the power to upgrade gear, not just the one placed near the Forge of Potential|
